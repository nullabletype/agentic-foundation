# Discovery brief

Status: draft / agreed for prototype / refined and accepted

## Problem and scope

- Who is using this, and what are they trying to achieve?
- What happens today? What specific outcome would improve it?
- Constraints: privacy, offline/network use, accessibility, deployment, time/cost.
- Non-goals: what are we explicitly leaving out?
- What evidence would make us stop or change direction?

## Scenarios and open questions

Describe concrete inputs, actions and expected outcomes. Include failure/recovery and important edge cases. Record which assumptions need a prototype and which require technical research. Ask follow-up questions that could change scope or architecture.
